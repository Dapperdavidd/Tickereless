# Brand marks

`AAPL, NVDA, META, TSLA, SPOT, AMZN, GOOGL` are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)
(simple-icons@13), released under CC0 1.0. `MSFT` is the four-square Microsoft
mark, authored here because Simple Icons removed it on trademark request.

The CC0 licence covers the SVG files, **not** the marks themselves. Each logo
remains the trademark of its owner, and this app shows them to identify the
company a discovery resolves to — nominative use, not endorsement. Before this
ships, each company's brand guidelines need review: several restrict use in
financial products, and every one of them prohibits any presentation implying
affiliation.
